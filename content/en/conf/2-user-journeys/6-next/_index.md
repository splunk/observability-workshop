{{% exercise title="Create a funnel" %}}
 1. Go to the Analyses tab and find the Journey Map that shows all journeys in this application.
 1. Select a path you're interested in and then click `Create funnel`.
 1. Rename the funnel, change the step labels as appropriate, and save. Now you've made your own funnel!
