## Sign In to Splunk Observability Cloud

To access Splunk Observability Cloud, navigate to 
[https://app.us1.signalfx.com/#/home`](https://app.us1.signalfx.com/#/home) using your browser. 

Use the credentials provided by the workshop organizer to sign in. 

After signing in, ensure that the `Observability Workshop AMER` org is selected.  Then, you should see a screen 
similar to the following example: 

![Splunk Observability Cloud Initial Screen](../../images/SplunkObservabilityCloud.png)
